WELCOME TO AL-MEESAQ

A school built around excellence, character and opportunity.

AL-MEESAQ HIGH SCHOOL is a learning community for boys and girls from Montessori to Matric. Our approach combines strong academics with a supportive environment that helps students become confident, responsible and capable individuals.

We believe education is more than examination success. It is about developing curiosity, discipline, communication, values and the confidence to face the future.
